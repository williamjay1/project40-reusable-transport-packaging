# Scientific claim → evidence → reproduction scope

This map distinguishes verified conditional calculations from input provenance and
unperformed empirical validation. The package is not an observed EU fleet dataset.

| Claim / number | New computation and frozen comparison | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 578 lifecycle policies | `code/run_lifecycle_v38.py`; `benchmarks/lifecycle_pilot_rows.json` | Declared jobs and old states, matched ownership options |
| 289 ownership pairs; 94 strict gains; 195 equalities | `code/extend_lifecycle_v38.py`; `benchmarks/lifecycle_structural.json` | Scope of this full scenario grid, not an estimated population rate |
| 14 rounded-certificate gaps | `code/lifecycle_v38_path_verify.py`; pilot and path-verification output | Exact independent assignment and rational constraints, not every dual is tight |
| 486 formula rows; 324 state comparisons | extension producer and independent path verifier | Stated equal-spacing and admissible-support assumptions |
| Four-job foreground; avoided manufacture only under long life | `code/lifecycle_foreground_v38.py`; `benchmarks/foreground_bridge.json` | 1.2kg and 0.31kWh source scalars; no full modern LCIA |
| 490 proxy distances; 471 retained positive arcs | `historical/inputs/geometry/*`; audit and historical runner | 19 exact-zero distances forbidden; no fleet validation |
| Adaptive dedicated/shared comparison | `historical/code/calendar_operating_thresholds_v23.py`; frozen `strong_baseline_comparisons.csv` | Same synthetic service and transport geometry |
| Phase and periodic-stock results | historical phase/periodic producers and verifiers; full benchmark CSVs | Periodic closure tested; no observed seasonality |
| Same-policy finance and environmental budgets | historical financial/collection/joint producers and independent verifiers | Partial cash boundary and design priors, not contracts or frequentist confidence |
| Ten figure sets | two redraw entry points; `figure_data/` and reference vectors | Numeric records retained; newly rendered raster/vector hashes may differ |

The full historical wrapper runs 24 producer/verifier stages. Every retained benchmark
CSV is compared; adverse/equality/failed-status rows are not dropped. Optimal witnesses
are checked by constraints rather than demanding one solver-selected degenerate path.

Frozen source extraction inputs are attributed, unit-labelled and immutable, but the
public runner does not reconstruct them from copyrighted source PDFs. Broad retired
seafood table reconstruction and old zero-arc control archives are not bundled or
re-executed. The positive-arc current policies and primary lifecycle claims are the
reproduction targets. Consult cited original sources for documentary details; do not
describe this package as recomputing every historical draft or validating all EU flows.

No linked asset IDs, ownership histories, entry ages, ready-return times, censoring or
retirement observations are supplied. Thus no survival distribution, calibrated loss
hazard, empirical asset-lifespan or present-day EU-27 effect can be claimed from this
repository. These limitations remain necessary even after a successful software run.
