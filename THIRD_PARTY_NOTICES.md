# Attribution, redistribution and scope

Audit date: 2026-10-05. This is a provenance/permissions screen, not a blanket
legal certification or a grant of rights held by third parties.

1. **Tua et al.,2019, Resources8(2),110; DOI10.3390/resources8020110.** The original
   article's copyright notice states CC BY4.0. Attributed numeric extracts of the article
   and supplementary source-response tables are retained, including printed rounding
   intervals and stage boundaries. No underlying ecoinvent database is redistributed.
   References: <https://doi.org/10.3390/resources8020110>;
   <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.
2. **R3PACK WP3 dataset D3.5**, creators Riccardo Accorsi, Giorgia Bartolotti, Beatrice
   Guidani, Riccardo Manzini and Michele Ronzoni; DOI10.5281/zenodo.21392137. The anonymous
   Zenodo API returned `license.id=cc-by-4.0` on the audit date. Only the attributed
   geometry excerpt and schema metadata are included, not the workbook. This does not
   grant permission to redistribute ecoinvent or assert that its food-packaging network
   is an observed crate pool. <https://zenodo.org/records/21392137>.
3. **JRC,2026 electricity generation factors**, Joana Bastos and Luana Valentini,
   DOI10.2905/JRC.PWGK5H0. The JRC landing page explicitly identifies CC BY4.0, anonymous
   access and temporal coverage1990–2024. The unchanged XLSX snapshot is retained for
   deterministic extraction of the Table3_LC_GHG2024 EU-27 column. Citation and reuse
   notice: <https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/0681adfd-9291-4b58-838a-67664b6d210f>.
4. **Eurostat nrg_pc_205**, DOI10.2908/NRG_PC_205. The small frozen JSON-stat extract is
   attributed to Eurostat. Its source update precedes the scientific cutoff. It contains
   aggregate EU-27 prices, not personal microdata. The separately labelled gas snapshot
   is after the cutoff and is **excluded** from estimates; it is retained only to make
   the rejection decision auditable. No source is represented as author-owned.
   The current copyright notice, fetched on 2026-10-05, permits reuse of statistical
   data with acknowledgement, subject to source-specific exceptions. These aggregate
   energy-price extracts are not the named commercial-use-restricted country/trade
   exceptions. Retain attribution rather than assigning a new author-owned licence.
   Transformations and research conclusions are solely the study authors' responsibility;
   Eurostat neither endorses nor is responsible for them:
   <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice>.
5. **Ceballos-Santos et al.,2024**, DOI10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.175452. The inspected
   original article explicitly states **CC BY-NC-ND**. Its PDF, full inventory table,
   supplement, figure artwork and reconstructed text are excluded. Four necessary
   attributed scalar facts (gross crate mass and production/wash resource amounts) are
   recorded with precise locators and the contextual washing-column ambiguity. No new
   licence is assigned to this source content. The author-created lifecycle example is
   separate; these four facts do not reconstruct a complete materials/energy/LCIA vector.
   <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.175452>.
6. **IFEU/EUPC2025 report; European Reuse Barometer2025; R3PACK D3.4 assumptions.** The
   original reports and copyrighted chart artwork are excluded. Small attributed
   scenario facts/bin counts are used only for definitions and limitations. Industry
   average cycles and format-level entries cannot be interpreted as individual asset
   lifetimes, linked cohorts or calibrated return probabilities. See `DATA_SOURCES.csv`.
7. **SciencePlots**, <https://github.com/garrettj403/SciencePlots>, commit
   `b9b16959570bd2fbc9ff5118bacc423c3bddd592`. Three pinned style files retain their
   original MIT licence in `code/vendor/scienceplots_v39/LICENSE`. No Nature endorsement
   or ownership of Nature's guide is implied. Only guidance is referenced; journal
   logos/templates are not copied.

Author licences, when approved, apply only to original software and original scenario
descriptions/results. Keep all source notices with reused extracts. Do not add a licence
badge or a universal CC BY statement that erases mixed rights. No vendor runtime,
Arial font file, commercial solver, licensed LCA process database or hidden account
credential is distributed.
