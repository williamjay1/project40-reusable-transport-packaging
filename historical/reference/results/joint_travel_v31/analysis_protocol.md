# Continuous joint transport-budget protocol

Recorded on 2026-10-01 before this extension is calculated. This is a disclosed post-result engineering extension, not a preregistration or an independent operating dataset.

Question: at what uniform empty-transport multiplier does a fixed, terminal-aware pooled policy stop providing both all-indicator source-accounting advantage against single use and included financial advantage against the equally adaptive dedicated policy, with at least 95% coverage under the existing energy design prior?

Inputs and policy freeze: the 56 independently checked v29 financial schedules, their actual cash ledger, the public-source Tua rounding constraints, the existing 432 v30 integrated contexts, and independent triangular(1,1,2) electricity/heat input multipliers. Removing the existing gamma=1/3 coordinate should yield 216 distinct price/fate/financial contexts; this is checked, not assumed silently. No new observations, tariffs, probability fitting, fleet optimisation or hygiene-performance claim are introduced.

Matrix: every distinct context, extra net pooling charge kappa=0 and a declared sensitivity kappa=0.005 EUR per discounted service. The latter is a hypothetical charge, not a measured pooling fee. All flat and adverse-loss/fate cases remain in the output. Calculate the environmental-only continuous threshold on these SAME policies as a matched comparator, not thresholds transplanted from the older reference-cost schedules.

For gamma>=0, integrate the declared density 4(1-u)(1-h) on [0,1]^2 over all environmental half-planes plus the differential cash-saving plane. Environment uses the exact sign-dependent replacement of source return transport, with t=(gamma*M/139-W)/100 and both positive printed transport endpoints retained. Financial saving is [B_D-B_P+(gamma-1)(B_T,D-B_T,P)]/Npv-e*u-f*h. Financial quantities alone are discounted. Existing rounding-only flat-policy differences are treated as cost neutrality after component checks, not as profit amplified by gamma.

Check positive transport endpoints and nonpositive differential transport slopes before using monotone inversion. Find the supremum gamma with coverage>=0.95 by a bounded doubling bracket and bisection. If coverage at zero is below 0.95, report no nonnegative joint transport budget (blank numeric root), never a negative physical distance or clipped zero. Constant strict cost ties fail. Retain roots below one, and report complete classifications rather than selecting favourable cases.

Translate gamma to gamma*M/N, with M the sum of dirty and clean empty crate-km including the stated clean-leg factor, and N delivered crate services. The unit is empty crate-km per delivered crate-service, not a single return-to-wash arc, loaded truck kilometres, a feasible geographic expansion, or a universal EU transport radius. Fleet, flows, return time, truck loading/technology, electricity/heat response and all other boundaries stay fixed. Longer distances with the same return deadline may be operationally infeasible; this is an exposure budget, not a logistics-plan certificate.

Validation: reproduce every existing gamma=1/3 integrated probability; analytic monotone/root and strict-tie controls; independent source constraint/cash-ledger reconstruction and vertical-slice quadrature, with a distinct root implementation; check root direction and retained all-context records; reaggregate crate-km from flow records.
